Before you start

The questions students scribble in the margin.

?Is EduCorps really free?

Yes. Notes, textbooks and past papers cost nothing. The only thing you bring is your own Groq key, which marks your papers on your side — so there’s no bill from us.

?Where does my Groq key go?

It stays in your browser, full stop. We never send it to our servers — the marking happens between you and Groq.

?Which subjects are covered?

Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Economics and Computer Science — each with its own notes, textbooks and a stack of 2025 past papers ready to solve.
